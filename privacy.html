JobSearch - Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

JobSearch is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): messages related to job applications.
  • Gmail (send): a plain-text reply to one recruiter email, sent only after the owner explicitly approves that exact text.
  • Google Calendar: reading events, and creating events only in the app's own "JobSearch" calendar.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed and stored only locally on the owner's computer. It is not sold or used for advertising. The only exception to local processing: when the owner asks, the text of a selected email may be passed to an AI assistant the owner uses, to draft a reply. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
